Rules used by this tool
  • Start: a cleanup always starts on the first day of the tax year (calendar or fiscal). It can't start mid-year, and you can't clean part of a closed tax year. (slides 15, 56)
  • PY: sold Jan 1–Aug 31. 2026 purchases: through Sep 30 (9/30 counts). Same calendar dates for fiscal-year filers. Covers all 12 months of the tax year that most recently closed, except short-period returns. Fixed end. (slides 12, 14, 15)
  • CY: sold May 1–Dec 31 for all filers. Covers the start of the tax year in progress through the month before purchase (1–11 months). Fixed end. (slides 12, 14, 15)
  • PY + CY: sold May 1–Aug 31. 2026 purchases: through Sep 30 (9/30 counts). Same calendar dates for fiscal-year filers. Covers the start of the prior tax year through the month before purchase, up to 20 months. Two cleanups in one engagement. Enter the entire period in Info Gathering. (slides 12, 95, 161)
  • Full Service: sold all year. Covers the start of a tax year through the month before onboarding at a minimum, and can be extended. Up to 20 months. Only Full Service goes to monthly. (slides 14, 15)
  • Full Service going-to-monthly minimum end: the later of (a) the month before onboarding and (b) the month-end 3 months before the doc review approval, expected cleanup completion (today + 30 or 14 days), or cleanup completion date. (from the Cleanup Period Calculator)
  • A filed return doesn't block a cleanup: a year with a filed return can still be cleaned if the client agrees to the disclosure (ToS effective 1/20/2026). It's treated as a SAM at doc review. CY can be cleaned while PY is unfiled, as long as no one is actively working the unfiled year. (slide 15)
  • SCU timelines: not onboarded by day 60 → refunded and closed. Day 120 → goes to cleanup even if docs are missing. 180-day cleanup entitlement. (slides 96–98)
